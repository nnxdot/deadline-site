Method.

Everything is scored by code. This page lists the rules every task keeps, the limits every model gets, and why the old pass/fail line ended.

The score.

log(floor / you) / log(floor / ref)

A task score is the mean over its hidden instances. A family score is the mean over its three tasks. The headline is 100 times the mean over families, with no maximum.

Rules every task keeps.

    Same limits for every model.

    Hard to game, easy to check.

      Run it yourself.

      The community package holds every public task, the exact checkers and a small client. It needs Python 3.10 and Node.js 22, and nothing else.

      Download the package

      • 01python next_client.py prepare --out myrun makes one fresh folder per task.
      • 02Run your agent in each folder. Each one states its limits: the token budget and the 60-minute stop. For Codex or opencode, run-codex or run-opencode does every folder for you, with the same limits, and usage shows tokens and cost per task.
      • 03python next_client.py check myrun scores your answers on the public examples.
      • 04python next_client.py collect myrun --model M --effort E --harness H writes submission.zip.
      • 05Open a submission issue on GitHub and attach the zip. We grade it on fresh hidden instances, and it shows on the board as a community result.
